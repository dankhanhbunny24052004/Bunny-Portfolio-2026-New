1. Let’s be a “sành ăn” Saigonese

Interactive Coding Project, 2026
Individual project

A playful food-discovery website helping users explore Saigon’s cuisine through random recommendations, from popular local dishes to hidden neighborhood spots.

Overview:

Let’s be a “sành ăn” Saigonese turns exploring local food into a playful digital experience, guiding users through dishes, neighbourhood finds, and everyday Saigon eating culture.

INTERACTIVE CODING / LET’S BE A “SÀNH ĂN” SAIGONESE

CONCEPT:

Inspired by the joy of sharing a good meal, the website makes food discovery feel spontaneous, colourful, and rooted in the neighbourhoods of Saigon.

HIGHLIGHTS:

  1. Location-aware food discovery. With the user’s permission, the website uses their current location to suggest nearby places serving the dish they land on.
  2. Social reviews in one place. Users can discover other people’s reviews and personal takes on each dish through social-media content.
  3. A playful streak to return for. The streak system makes daily food discovery feel rewarding and encourages users to keep coming back.
