1. PECKY

2D Graphical Animation, 2025
Individual project

A 2D animation exploring blind-box obsession through Pecky’s escalating pursuit of a rare collectible.

Overview:

Pecky is a 2D graphical animation that follows a young collector’s increasingly obsessive pursuit of her favorite blind-box character. Through a playful and exaggerated narrative, the project captures the excitement, disappointment, and emotional highs that come with repeated unboxing.

2D GRAPHICAL ANIMATION / PECKY

CONCEPT:

The concept explores how blind-box culture can turn anticipation and reward into a repetitive cycle of desire. Inspired by the popularity of collectible toys and unboxing trends, the animation uses Pecky’s escalating behavior to reflect the addictive appeal of surprise-based consumption in a humorous and visually expressive way.
