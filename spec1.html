1. After all

Interactive Campaign, 2025
Individual project

An interactive online ritual that reimagines joss paper burning in a digital form, preserving tradition while reducing smoke and environmental impact.

Overview:

After all reimagines the joss-paper burning ritual as a digital experience, inviting people to reflect on tradition, memory, and environmental impact through interaction.

INTERACTIVE CAMPAIGN / AFTER ALL

CONCEPT:

The project uses an interactive online ritual to preserve the cultural meaning of joss-paper burning while reducing smoke and environmental impact.

HIGHLIGHT:

These clips highlight well-known celebrations in Vietnam. Each occasion involves burning a different type of joss paper, resulting in distinct visual effects.
