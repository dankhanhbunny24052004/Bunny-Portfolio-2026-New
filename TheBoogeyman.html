This is a sound design project for a fictional video game that incorporates the concepts we developed. Video game The Bogeyman is a 2.5D horror, adventure, and puzzle-solving game that tells a story. The players assume the role of A Xìn, a 10-year-old child who is tasked with overcoming adversities and impediments in order to rescue his companions who have been abducted by the Boogeyman. The fundamental recordings were distributed equally among the members of the ensemble. Hung was responsible for the creation of ambient sequences, synthesized noises, and composite and processed sounds. Khanh was responsible for the voice recordings, music mood board, presentation transparencies, and portions of the design document. Dung was responsible for the sketching and design of some of the design document.The primary objective of this project is to illustrate and accentuate the cultural diversity of Vietnam, including folklore, traditional games and music, mythology, and legends, through the use of immersive video game sound design.
