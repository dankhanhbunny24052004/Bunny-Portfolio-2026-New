1. Undercover vendor

3D Character Design, 2025
Individual project

An antihero character project exploring an undercover police officer disguised as a street fishball vendor.

Overview:

Uncle Minh is a 3D character design project centered on an anti-hero disguised as a street fish-ball vendor. The project explores the contrast between an ordinary neighborhood figure and the hidden identity of an undercover police officer, turning a familiar piece of Vietnamese street culture into a playful character narrative.

(MODELING, TEXTURING, ANIMATION) / UNDERCOVER VENDOR

CONCEPT:

Inspired by local fish-ball stalls and online jokes about street vendors secretly being undercover cops, Uncle Minh reimagines an ordinary neighborhood figure as a covert protector hiding in plain sight.
