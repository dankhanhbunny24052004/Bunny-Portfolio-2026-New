Thuyen va bien

_Xuan Quynh_

The boy in the poem symbolizes the boat, while the girl represents the sea, encapsulating the depth, gentleness, and magical hues of a woman’s mind. At times, the sea can be gentle, guiding the boat to a variety of locations. However, it can also be fierce, pushing the boat without evident reason, thereby expressing the complex emotions of a girl in love.
