1. JESTER

Mixed-Media Stop-Motion, 2026

A playful mixed-media stop-motion animation about a toy searching for acceptance and belonging, celebrating the idea of being valued for one’s authentic self.

Overview:

Jester is a mixed-media stop-motion animation about a toy who continues performing in hopes of being chosen, only to remain unnoticed as the other toys gradually find new homes. Through an unexpected encounter with someone who appreciates his uniqueness, Jester finally experiences the acceptance and belonging he has been searching for.

MIXED-MEDIA STOP-MOTION / JESTER

CONCEPT:

The project explores belonging, individuality, and the desire to be accepted without changing oneself to fit in. By using a playful toy-store setting and handcrafted mixed-media visuals, the story contrasts Jester’s cheerful performances with his growing exhaustion, ultimately framing authenticity and being valued for one’s differences as the emotional core of the film.
