Overview:

A collaborative VJ project reimagining Vietnamese urban legends through a water-based street world inspired by Cần Thơ and the Mekong Delta.

VISUAL JOCKEY / LINH DỊ VINA

CONCEPT:

Blending uncanny folklore, childhood memory, joss paper imagery, and river landscapes, the project creates a nostalgic yet unfamiliar visual world that feels distinctly “Vina.”
