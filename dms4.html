1. “Nâu” — A smart grocery website

AI Assisted Web Platform, 2026
Individual project

A smart grocery website designed to help users plan meals, find the ingredients they need, and make better use of leftover food at home.

Overview:

“Nâu” — A Smart Grocery Website is an interactive grocery platform designed to simplify meal planning and ingredient shopping. The website helps users discover recipes from ingredients they already have, generate shopping lists based on selected meals and serving sizes, and revisit previous searches for easier planning.

AI ASSISTED WEB PLATFORM / “NÂU” — A SMART GROCERY WEBSITE

Click here to experience the website.

CONCEPT:

The project was developed around the idea of making grocery shopping more intelligent, efficient, and less wasteful. By connecting recipe discovery, ingredient scanning, automatic quantity calculation, and cart generation in one experience, “Nâu” supports users in making better use of available food, avoiding overbuying, and planning meals with less effort.

HIGHLIGHTS:

The website combines two online-grocery functions to create a more engaging user experience.

FUNCTION 1 — MEAL-BASED INGREDIENT PLANNING & AUTOMATIC CART GENERATION

Users enter the meal they want to cook, serving size, and dietary needs. The system then calculates the required ingredients and adds the appropriate quantities to the cart automatically.

FUNCTION 2 — SCAN INGREDIENTS FOR MEAL SUGGESTIONS

Users can scan the ingredients they already have, and the system suggests suitable recipes based on those ingredients, helping them make better use of available food and reduce waste.
