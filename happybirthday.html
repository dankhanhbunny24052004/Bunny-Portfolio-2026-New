Dung, Hung and I are responsible for the development and production of sound for a short horror film. In this film, a girl is killed on her birthday by a cursed cake that contains a multiplicity of razor blades within its interior. She was initially under the impression that her mother had made the cake; however, it was the preternatural faction in her chamber that was responsible. We all recorded sounds from items in Dung's home and incorporated synthesized sounds.
