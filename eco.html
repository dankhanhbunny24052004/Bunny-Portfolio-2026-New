PUBLIC TRANSPORT CAMPAIGN & VISUAL DESIGN / ECO-LINK CITY

CONCEPT:

A visual campaign designed to make public transport more visible and accessible, introducing metro, buses, public bicycles, and water buses through clear and engaging imagery.

CAMPAIGN RESEARCH

The city is moving forward—but public habits are slower to follow.

Ho Chi Minh City is rapidly expanding its multimodal public transport system, including metro lines, buses, public bicycles, and water buses. While this development is creating the foundation for a more sustainable mobility network, public travel habits have not yet changed at the same pace.

This image presents a redesigned intersection in Lima, Peru, transformed through the work of artist Carlos Cruz-Diez as part of the Color Aditivo project. The intervention was intended to encourage drivers to slow down and become more alert when approaching dangerous intersections.

  • Design: Instead of relying on standard white road markings, the project uses bright color fields and varied geometric forms to create a more noticeable streetscape.
  • Impact: The project demonstrates how public art can contribute to safer urban environments while also making city spaces more visually engaging.

Located in the heart of the city, September 23rd Park sits near major transport links and popular tourist areas. Its consistently high pedestrian flow makes it an effective site for communicating public transport information to a broad and diverse audience.

Through on-site research and observation, our team identified several key drawbacks of the location, including spatial limitations, waste accumulation, unpleasant odors, and inappropriate public behavior. These findings helped us better understand the site conditions and informed how we approached the design solution.
